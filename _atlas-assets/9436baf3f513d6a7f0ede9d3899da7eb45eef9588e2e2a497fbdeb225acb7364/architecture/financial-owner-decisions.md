# Approved financial governance — September 21, 2026

These owner decisions supersede the pending questions in the original audit. Approval of the rules is not evidence that all rules have been implemented or accepted.

- Monthly close authority: Accounting-approved Budget Comparison Income Statement; Trial Balance is a control when supplied. PDF is the primary format, native text before OCR. A matching XLSX is another representation of the same package, not another actuals publication.
- ATLAS performs technical validation, approved property mapping, GL mapping, reconciliation and import review; it does not repeat Accounting's approval.
- Only active Admin users may close, reopen, replace closed actuals, publish financial data, or activate a reforecast comparison baseline. Other scoped roles may perform their authorized preparation and review work.
- Ruston is an approved alias for The Preserve at Tech. Community Settings controls Student Housing classification and fiscal calendars. Never keep a second permanent community-name list in the finance parser.
- Original approved budgets are immutable and remain the normal comparison baseline. Revised budgets, reforecast vintages, commitments, adjustments and actual-close versions remain distinct. Reforecast activation records Admin, reason, time, version and periods and preserves the original comparison.
- A closed replacement retains the previous version, source/hash, reason, totals before/after, GL differences, actor/time, recalculation state and recipient-resolved notices.
- Favorability follows configured financial nature, not the amount's sign. Higher income/GPR/NOI/cash flow and lower expenses are favorable. Contra-income classifications must be explicit.
- Default GL warning: $2,500 AND 10%; critical: $10,000 OR 20%. Aggregate GPR/income/expenses/NOI warning: $5,000 AND 5%. Warn after two consecutive unfavorable closed periods. Settings are editable by authorized scope and copied into each generated alert.
- Leasing bonus measurement is monthly; other bonus measurement is quarterly. Payable runs require approved closed matching periods, community, assignment, plan and eligibility. Preserve actual/target sources, metric definition, formula, attainment, payout and calculation version. Include capital, debt, below-NOI, intercompany and noncontrollable accounts according to their classification and plan, never blanket exclusion.
- Financial/operational/contract task recommendations require user acceptance before assignment. Manual and accepted recommended tasks share audit, edit, owner, due date, completion, separate verification, report and export behavior.
- Resolve notification recipients from current affected Regional and Central Services assignments. No hardcoded names. Financial-detail access follows community scope for authenticated users; suppress resident, bank, tax, credential and unnecessary personal data. Leadership/investor reports may contain explanatory GL detail.

## Conservice contracts

Conservice is authoritative. There is no automatic integration. The signed manual upload must include the attestation: “I confirm this is the current signed contract obtained from Conservice.” Record canonical community, vendor/service, version, signed/effective dates, renewal/termination/notice provisions, pricing/escalation, GL mapping, uploader/time, source file/hash, confidence, reviewer and user-verification date.

Regionals, Admin or Central Services approve extracted terms. Unapproved proposals do not affect published budgets, forecasts, alerts or payable bonuses. Keep immutable prior versions and change only the active pointer. Future-approved documents wait until their effective date. Older, duplicate, unsigned, unapproved or conflicting uploads cannot replace active terms. Same-term conflicts require review.

Lifecycle: Uploaded → Extraction Review → Approved/Future or Approved/Active → Expiring → Expired or Superseded. Rejected and Conflict Review are explicit states. Audit every transition. Configurable 180/120/90/60/30-day alerts distinguish notice deadlines, expiration, awaiting upload/review/activation, unmapped GLs and conflicts. Display user-verified freshness; “Verify against Conservice” is a controlled human action, never an automatic freshness claim.
